## Sanity's Edge Overview
- Sanity's Edge is a 12-player trial in the Telvanni Peninsula, introduced with the Necrom Chapter (2023).
- Players enter the dreamworld/nightmare of Warlock Vanton, trapped by the Daedra Ansuul the Tormentor.
- Dream/nightmare themed mechanics with puzzle elements.

### Bosses (in encounter order)
1. **Trash Pulls** — Disruptors, Voidmasters, Summoners, Butchers, Enforcers
2. **Exarchanic Yaseyla** (Boss 1) — Horror adds, Vanton's Clarity mechanic
3. **Archwizard Twelvane** (Boss 2) — Crystal sequences, Chimera add
4. **Ansuul the Tormentor** (Final Boss) — Torment portals, maze mechanic, multi-phase

### Trifecta: Master of the Mind
Requires in a single Veteran run: all HM banners raised, zero deaths, under 30 minutes.
